Speaking Section Overview

The Speaking section tests your ability to speak English clearly. Focus on fluency, pronunciation, and content.

General Speaking Tips

  • Speak at a natural pace - not too fast, not too slow
  • Use proper intonation and stress patterns
  • Don't restart - keep going even if you make mistakes
  • Practice with a microphone daily

1. Read Aloud (6-7 items)

▼

Task: Read a text aloud (30-40 seconds preparation, 40 seconds to read)

Strategies

  • Use prep time to identify difficult words
  • Mark natural pauses at commas and periods
  • Stress important words (nouns, verbs, adjectives)
  • Maintain steady pace throughout

Practice Passages

Climate change represents one of the most significant challenges facing humanity today. Scientists have documented rising global temperatures, melting ice caps, and increasingly severe weather patterns. The consequences of inaction could be catastrophic for future generations.

The development of artificial intelligence has transformed numerous industries, from healthcare to transportation. Machine learning algorithms can now diagnose diseases, predict market trends, and even create artistic works. However, these advances also raise important ethical questions.

Universities play a crucial role in advancing knowledge and preparing students for professional careers. Through research and teaching, academic institutions contribute to economic development and social progress in communities worldwide.

2. Repeat Sentence (10-12 items)

▼

Task: Listen and repeat exactly what you hear

Strategies

  • Focus on meaning, not just individual words
  • Start speaking immediately after audio ends
  • If you forget part, say what you remember confidently
  • Maintain the same intonation as original

Practice Sentences (memorize then repeat)

"The assignment must be submitted by the end of the week."

"Students should reference their sources in the bibliography."

"The research findings have significant implications for public health."

"Economic growth depends on innovation and sustainable practices."

"The library offers extensive resources for academic research."

3. Describe Image (3-4 items)

▼

Task: Describe an image in 40 seconds (25s prep time)

Template

  • Intro: "This image/graph shows..."
  • Key feature: "The most significant feature is..."
  • Details: "We can also see that..."
  • Trends: "There is an increase/decrease in..."
  • Conclusion: "In conclusion, this image demonstrates..."

4. Re-tell Lecture (1-2 items)

▼

Task: Listen to lecture, take notes, re-tell in 40 seconds

Note-Taking Tips

  • Write keywords, not full sentences
  • Note the main topic immediately
  • Capture 3-4 key points
  • Listen for examples

Speaking Template

"The lecture discusses [TOPIC]. The speaker explains that [POINT 1]. Additionally, [POINT 2]. Furthermore, [POINT 3]. In conclusion, [SUMMARY]."

Writing Section Overview

The Writing section tests your ability to write academic English. Two task types: Summarize Written Text and Write Essay.

1. Summarize Written Text (1-2 items)

▼

Task: Write ONE sentence summary (10 minutes, 5-75 words)

Key Strategies

  • Identify main idea + 2-3 supporting points
  • Use connectors: which, that, while, although, because
  • Keep it as ONE grammatically correct sentence
  • Aim for 30-50 words
  • Paraphrase - don't copy directly

Useful Connectors

which that while whereas although because therefore however moreover consequently

Practice Task

Social media has revolutionized the way people communicate and share information. Platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram have connected billions of users worldwide, enabling instant communication across geographical boundaries. However, this digital revolution has also raised concerns about privacy, mental health, and the spread of misinformation. Studies have shown that excessive social media use can lead to anxiety, depression, and decreased attention spans, particularly among young users.

Words: 0/75

2. Write Essay (1-2 items)

▼

Task: Write 200-300 word essay (20 minutes)

Essay Structure

  • Introduction (40-50 words): Hook + Thesis
  • Body 1 (70-90 words): Topic + Explanation + Example
  • Body 2 (70-90 words): Topic + Explanation + Example
  • Conclusion (40-50 words): Restate + Final thought

Reading Section Overview

The Reading section tests comprehension and vocabulary. Time management is crucial!

1. Re-order Paragraphs (2-3 items)

▼

Task: Arrange sentences in correct order

Key Strategies

  • Find topic sentence: No pronouns referring back, introduces main idea
  • Follow pronouns: "This", "These", "It" refer to something earlier
  • Linking words: However, Therefore, Moreover, Furthermore
  • Articles: "The" = already mentioned, "A/An" = new concept

Practice

Arrange in correct order:

A) However, this convenience comes with environmental costs.
B) Online shopping has revolutionized consumer purchasing.
C) Furthermore, product returns add to the carbon footprint.
D) The increase in delivery vehicles contributes to pollution.

2. Fill in the Blanks (4-5 items)

▼

Task: Select correct words from dropdown or drag-drop

Strategies

  • Read entire sentence before choosing
  • Check collocations (words that go together)
  • Consider grammar: verb forms, prepositions
  • Use context clues

Common Collocations

make a decision
NOT do a decision
pay attention
NOT give attention
conduct research
NOT make research
raise awareness
NOT lift awareness
reach a conclusion
NOT arrive conclusion
take responsibility
NOT make responsibility

3. Multiple Choice (1-2 items)

▼

Task: Answer questions about a passage

Tips

  • Read question first, then passage
  • Eliminate obviously wrong answers
  • For multiple answers: be careful - wrong selections lose points

Practice

The Industrial Revolution transformed society from agrarian to industrial. Factories replaced cottage industries, steam power revolutionized production, and urban centers grew rapidly. While bringing economic growth, it also created social challenges including poor working conditions and pollution.

What was the main impact of the Industrial Revolution?

A) It improved agricultural practices
B) It transformed society from agricultural to industrial
C) It only affected Britain
D) It eliminated all social problems

Listening Section Overview

Audio plays ONCE only! Concentration and note-taking are crucial.

General Tips

  • Take notes - keywords, not full sentences
  • Focus on main ideas and supporting points
  • Listen for signal words: however, therefore, importantly
  • Practice with British, American, and Australian accents

1. Summarize Spoken Text (1-2 items)

▼

Task: Listen to lecture, write 50-70 word summary (10 minutes)

Note Template

  • Topic: _______
  • Point 1: _______
  • Point 2: _______
  • Point 3: _______
  • Conclusion: _______

Writing Template

"The lecture discusses [TOPIC]. The speaker explains that [POINT 1]. Additionally, [POINT 2] is highlighted. Furthermore, [POINT 3]. In conclusion, [SUMMARY]."

2. Write from Dictation (3-4 items) - VERY IMPORTANT!

▼

Task: Type exactly what you hear

This task heavily impacts both Listening AND Writing scores!

Strategies

  • Write keywords first, fill gaps after
  • Focus on articles (a, an, the) and prepositions
  • Check spelling carefully
  • Each correct word = 1 point
