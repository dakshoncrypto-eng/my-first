## Trending Formats to Leverage

### Format 1: "Rating" Videos
Rate peptide claims on evidence quality (1-10 scale with explanations)
**Why it works:** Satisfying, shareable, positions you as authority

### Format 2: "Green Screen Reaction"
React to viral peptide misinformation with calm, evidence-based corrections
**Why it works:** Controversy + value, algorithm loves duets/stitches

### Format 3: "3 Things" Lists
"3 peptide red flags", "3 questions to ask sellers", "3 claims that are BS"
**Why it works:** Digestible, screenshot-worthy, high completion rate

### Format 4: "Storytime"
"I spent 6 months researching peptides. Here's what I learned."
**Why it works:** Personal narrative, emotional connection, watch time

### Format 5: "POV/Skit"
Role-play conversations (seller vs informed buyer, gym bros, etc.)
**Why it works:** Entertaining, relatable, shareable

### Format 6: "Text on Screen + Voiceover"
Fast-paced information delivery with punchy text overlays
**Why it works:** Accessibility, rewatchability, easy to produce

### Format 7: "Duet/Stitch Corrections"
Find viral peptide misinformation, stitch it with evidence-based response
**Why it works:** Built-in audience, controversy engagement, authority building
